# **App Name**: Shreyash Solutions Corporate Site

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Dynamic product catalog for potentiometric sensors, fluid level sensors, Hall-effect sensors, and electromechanical assemblies.
- Service Showcase: Clearly present services including potentiometric sensor design, precision winding, and third-party inspection services.
- Capabilities Highlight: Visually showcase precision winding, PCB printing, testing equipment, and prototyping capabilities with images and descriptions.
- Competitive Edge Display: Emphasize domain expertise, military-grade quality, R&D capabilities, and cost advantages in a dedicated section.
- Industry Sections: Dedicated subsections for defense, automotive, industrial automation, and agriculture, highlighting relevant use cases.
- SEO Optimization: Generate SEO metadata based on product and service descriptions
- Inquiry & Quote Forms: Implement contact and request-a-quote forms to capture leads and inquiries with necessary data validation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Turquoise blue (#40E0D0) to convey trustworthiness and technical expertise.
- Background color: Off White (#F4F7F9) to provide a clean and minimalist backdrop.
- Accent color: Steel Grey (#5A6B7A) for call-to-action buttons and important highlights.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font, providing a modern, neutral aesthetic for both headlines and body text.
- Use technical icons for products and services. Ensure that the style is clean and consistent with the overall design.
- Design a high-trust layout with clear section headings and structured bullet points for easy information access.
- Subtle animations such as micro-interactions on button hover and smooth transitions between page sections to enhance user experience.
